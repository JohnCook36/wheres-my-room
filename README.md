# Where's My Room?

**Where's My Room?** is a first-person narrative adventure about the employees of a large city hotel.

The game combines:
- hotel workplace drama and comedy;
- guest-service gameplay;
- mystery and supernatural elements;
- exploration of public and back-of-house spaces;
- player choices and relationships with coworkers.

## Current concept

- Setting: fictional city
- Hotel size: about 300 active rooms
- Two guest wings
- Floors 2-6: active room inventory
- Floor 7: closed historical floor
- Room 713: central supernatural mystery
- Perspective: first person
- Target platform: PC / Windows
- Engine direction: Unreal Engine 5.8
- Visual direction: cinematic stylized realism

## Development goal

The first milestone is a **35-45 minute vertical slice of Shift 1**.

The prototype should include:
- lobby and Front Office;
- Back Office / BOH route;
- one playable guest-floor section;
- basic PMS interaction;
- check-in and key-card flow;
- phone and radio interactions;
- a guest-service task;
- the first appearance of Room 713 in the PMS.
